Objective

Develop a high-agility, autonomous micro-drone system named Musquiteer to detect, pursue, and neutralize mosquitoes indoors.

Operating Concept

Mosquitoes (Aedes / Culex) fly erratically at 1.2 – 2.5 m/s with accelerations exceeding 1.5 g. An effective interception system requires high thrust-to-weight ratio (>3:1), high-framerate optical tracking (≥100 FPS), and zero parasitic aerodynamic drag.

The system is partitioned into two specialized nodes:

  • Stationary Optical Base Station: High-framerate (120 FPS) Near-Infrared (NIR) camera with 850nm bandpass filter and active 850nm illumination. Tracks insect centroids via temporal differencing and predicts flight trajectories using a 4-state Kalman filter. Sends real-time guidance setpoints to the drone at 100 Hz over ESP-NOW (<2ms latency).
  • Agile Micro-Interceptor Drone: 65mm ducted brushless Whoop (32g All-Up Weight). High thrust-to-weight ratio (4.3:1) powered by 0802 19,000KV brushless motors. Hard real-time 500 Hz attitude control on an integrated ESP32-S3 All-In-One (AIO) flight controller.
  • Kinetic Ingestion Neutralization: Ducted propellers spinning at 28,000 RPM create an intake suction funnel that mechanically neutralizes mosquitoes upon contact—eliminating high-voltage hazards and EMI completely.
