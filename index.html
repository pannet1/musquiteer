Project at a glance

ItemDetail
GoalDevelop a compact four-motor indoor drone with an onboard camera and programmable electronics to detect and track mosquitoes. Long-term: a flat mosquito-bat-style mesh attachment; eventual autonomous operation without a handheld remote.
Drone platformLiteWing ESP32-S3 programmable micro-drone — indicative price ~’3,999
Camera / AI boardFireBeetle 2 ESP32-S3 camera board — indicative price ~’1,949
Indicative budget’7,648 total (’8,000 concept) — planning figures, not a verified quotation
Powered mesh on drone?No. Only a non-powered mesh mock-up is budgeted. An energized grid must never be mounted on the flying drone.
Project statusPreliminary experimental concept. First milestone: safe programmable flying platform plus a working camera feed.
⚠ Critical safety warning (read first): Do not mount an energized mosquito-racket grid or exposed high-voltage contacts on the flying drone. High voltage near propellers, wiring, and a lithium battery creates serious shock, short-circuit, and fire risks. The budget includes only a lightweight, non-powered mesh-shaped mock-up. Any powered mesh mechanism requires a separate guarded stationary prototype, independent electrical safety testing, and a new engineering review before integration is considered.
