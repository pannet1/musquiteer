2. Operational Limitations & Bounds

  • Flight Volume: A single wide-angle base camera covers an effective tracking space of approx. 3.0m × 3.0m × 2.5m.
  • Flight Endurance: 1S 300mAh LiHV batteries provide 3.5 to 4.5 minutes of continuous flight.
  • Ambient Sunlight: Direct sunlight through unshaded windows contains strong NIR energy; draw blinds during operation to maximize signal-to-noise ratio.
  • Ceiling Fans: Turn off high-speed ceiling fans to avoid turbulent downdrafts that exceed micro-quad attitude margins.
