1. Why High-Voltage Meshes Were Eliminated

Mounting an energized mosquito racket grid onto a micro-drone creates three fatal engineering failure modes:

Fatal Failure Modes of High Voltage on Micro-Drones:
  • Broadband EMI & MCU Latch-Up: Electric arcs generate nanosecond dI/dt transients that induce ground bounce, crashing the ESP32-S3 and freezing the IMU.
  • Aerodynamic Drag & Inertia: A planar mesh acts as a sail, causing uncontrolled pitching moments and sluggish PID response.
  • LiPo Fire Hazard: High-voltage arcs adjacent to a lightweight lithium cell present a severe fire risk during crashes.

2. The Selected Solution: Mechanical Propeller Ingestion

The engineered design uses the drone's existing propulsion system as the neutralization mechanism:

  • Blade Tip Velocity: At 28,000 RPM, the tips of the 31mm polycarbonate propellers travel at over 163 km/h (45.4 m/s).
  • Aerodynamic Inflow Funnel: The ducted cowl creates a low-pressure intake funnel that draws the insect into the spinning rotor disk when within 3 cm.
  • Kinetic Impact: Polycarbonate blades colliding at >160 km/h instantly neutralize the insect upon contact.
  • Zero Weight & Zero EMI: Adds 0g of mass and produces zero electromagnetic interference.
