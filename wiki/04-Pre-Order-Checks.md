# 4. Pre-Order Checks

**Source:** Section 4 of `Mosquito_Hunting_Drone_8K_Project_Summary.docx`

---

## Drone package contents

Confirm the drone package includes:

- Flight controller
- Four motors
- Propellers
- Frame
- Battery
- Charger
- Guards

## Flight controller

Ask whether the flight controller supports **external commands or sensor-data communication**.

## Payload

Obtain the drone's **safe payload limit** and confirm it can carry the camera board and mount.

## Camera board

Confirm:

- Camera-board dimensions
- Mass
- Voltage/current requirements
- Safe power arrangement

## Camera video

Confirm:

- How the camera video can be accessed by the AI software
- Whether its frame rate is adequate

## Connectors and power

Match:

- Connector type
- Wire gauge
- Battery connector
- Charger

…to the selected platform.

## Seller and logistics

Confirm:

- Current availability
- Shipping cost
- Return policy

## Ordering rule

**Do not order the boards until their physical, electrical, and software compatibility has been checked.**
