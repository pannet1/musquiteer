# 5. Prototype Capabilities

**Source:** Section 5 of `Mosquito_Hunting_Drone_8K_Project_Summary.docx`

---

## Planned capabilities

| Capability | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Four-motor flight | Platform-dependent | Test manually first |
| Onboard camera | After mechanical and electrical integration | — |
| Wireless video | Must be tested with selected camera/software | — |
| Mosquito detection | Experimental model | Collect and label suitable images |
| Autonomous tracking / interception | Future development | **Not** a proven included feature |
| Powered electric mesh | **Not included** on the flying prototype | See safety page |

---

## What is NOT included

- **Powered electric mesh** on the flying prototype
- Autonomous tracking/interception (future development only)
- Proven mosquito recognition
