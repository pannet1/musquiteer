## Important caveats

- These are **planning figures from the previous discussion**, not a verified live quotation or confirmed compatible bill of materials.
- Shipping, taxes, and seller prices may change the total.
- The ₹8,000 concept is a **preliminary experimental budget**, not a guarantee that a complete autonomous mosquito-hunting drone can be assembled for that amount.
