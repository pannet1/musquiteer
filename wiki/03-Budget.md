---

## Budget Notes

* **Cost vs. Original Planning Figure:** The revised total (₹9,300) is within ~15% of the original ₹8,000 concept budget, but delivers a genuine **brushless 4.3:1 thrust-to-weight platform** and a **120 FPS global-shutter optical tracking system**.
* **Zero Wasted Silicon:** Eliminating the FireBeetle 2 board (₹1,949) and the non-viable bat mesh mock-up reallocates funds directly into the brushless motors and the high-speed base camera.
* **Component Sourcing:** All parts are off-the-shelf items stocked by major Indian drone and electronics suppliers (Robu.in, QuadKopter.in, ElectronicsComp).
