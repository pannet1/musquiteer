## Project at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Goal** | Develop a compact four-motor indoor drone with an onboard camera and programmable electronics to detect and track mosquitoes. Long-term: a flat mosquito-bat-style mesh attachment; eventual autonomous operation without a handheld remote. |
| **Drone platform** | LiteWing ESP32-S3 programmable micro-drone — indicative price ~₹3,999 |
| **Camera/AI board** | FireBeetle 2 ESP32-S3 camera board — indicative price ~₹1,949 |
| **Indicative budget** | ₹7,648 total (₹8,000 concept) — planning figures, not a verified quotation |
| **Powered mesh on drone?** | **No.** Only a non-powered mesh mock-up is budgeted. An energized grid must never be mounted on the flying drone. |
| **Project status** | Preliminary experimental concept. First milestone: safe programmable flying platform + working camera feed. |

## Key safety warning (read first)

**Do not mount an energized mosquito-racket grid or exposed high-voltage contacts on the flying drone.** High voltage near propellers, wiring, and a lithium battery creates serious shock, short-circuit, and fire risks. The budget includes only a lightweight, non-powered mesh-shaped mock-up. Any powered mesh mechanism requires a separate guarded stationary prototype, independent electrical safety testing, and a new engineering review before integration is considered.
