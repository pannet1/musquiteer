# 8. Project Status & Limitations

**Source:** Section 8 of `Mosquito_Hunting_Drone_8K_Project_Summary.docx`

---

## Budget status

The ₹8,000 concept is a **preliminary experimental budget**, not a guarantee that a complete autonomous mosquito-hunting drone can be assembled for that amount.

## Verification status

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Proposed drone + camera board together | **Not verified** as compatible |
| Complete autonomous mosquito-hunting drone | Not yet engineered |
| ₹8,000 as a guaranteed total | Not guaranteed |

## First milestone

A **safe programmable flying platform** plus a **working camera feed**.

## Remaining engineering work

The following remain to be engineered and tested:

- Mosquito recognition
- Onboard AI autonomy
- Payload capacity
- Any elimination mechanism
