# 7. Recommended Build Sequence

**Source:** Section 7 of `Mosquito_Hunting_Drone_8K_Project_Summary.docx`

---

## Step 1 — Confirm before ordering

1. Ask the **drone seller** to confirm:
   - Package contents
   - Payload capacity
   - Battery inclusion
   - Supported programming interfaces
2. Ask the **camera-board seller** to confirm:
   - Weight
   - Dimensions
   - Power requirements
   - Video-stream access

**Do not order the boards until their physical, electrical, and software compatibility has been checked.**

---

## Step 2 — Test the drone platform alone

- Test the drone platform by itself in a **clear, controlled area**
- Follow the **manufacturer's instructions**

---

## Step 3 — Test the camera and AI board separately

- Test on a **bench**
- Verify **video capture**
- Verify **basic insect-image classification**

---

## Step 4 — Integrate the camera

Integrate the camera only after confirming:

- Safe power
- Safe mounting
- Payload limits

**Keep motors disabled during electronics tests.**

---

## Step 5 — Develop autonomy

Develop autonomous flight and tracking **only after**:

- Stable manual flight is demonstrated, **and**
- Reliable camera operation is demonstrated

---

## Mesh attachment rule

Keep the mesh attachment **non-powered during flight testing**.
