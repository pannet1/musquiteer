# 6. Mosquito-Bat Attachment & Safety

**Source:** Section 6 of `Mosquito_Hunting_Drone_8K_Project_Summary.docx`

---

## What the budget includes

The budget includes only a **lightweight, non-powered mesh-shaped mock-up** — a mosquito-bat-style attachment that is **not energized**.

---

## ⚠️ Critical safety warning

**Do not mount an energized mosquito-racket grid or exposed high-voltage contacts on the flying drone.**

High voltage near propellers, wiring, and a lithium battery creates serious:

- **Shock risks**
- **Short-circuit risks**
- **Fire risks**

---

## Requirements for any powered mesh

Any powered mesh mechanism requires, before integration is considered:

1. A **separate guarded stationary prototype**
2. **Independent electrical safety testing**
3. A **new engineering review**

---

## Flight testing rule

Keep the mesh attachment **non-powered during flight testing**.
