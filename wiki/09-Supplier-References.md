---

## Sourcing Guidelines

1. **Batch Delivery:** Order the airframe, motors, propellers, and AIO flight controller together to save on consolidated shipping.
2. **Connector Uniformity:** Ensure the batteries and charger use the same solid-pin connector standard (either BT2.0 or A30). Do not mix connectors.
