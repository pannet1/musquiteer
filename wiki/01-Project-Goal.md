# 1. Project Goal

**Source:** Section 1 of `Mosquito_Hunting_Drone_8K_Project_Summary.docx`

---

## Objective

Develop a compact **four-motor indoor drone** with:

- an **onboard camera**
- **programmable electronics**

…to **detect and track mosquitoes**.

---

## Long-term concept

- A **flat mosquito-bat-style mesh attachment**
- Eventual **autonomous operation** — without a handheld remote

---

## Important status note

The LiteWing ESP32-S3 is a **development platform, not a finished autonomous mosquito-hunting product**. The system described here is a preliminary concept. Mosquito recognition, onboard AI autonomy, payload capacity, and any elimination mechanism remain to be engineered and tested.
