3. Why the FireBeetle 2 Board Was Eliminated

  • Mass Penalty: The FireBeetle 2 board with camera lens and mounting hardware weighs ~18–22g. On a 35g drone, this is a 60% mass increase, causing immediate motor saturation and battery voltage collapse.
  • Silicon Redundancy: The drone flight controller already features an ESP32-S3. Carrying two identical MCUs doubles power regulators and passive losses needlessly.
  • Severe Motion Blur: The OV2640 rolling-shutter sensor indoor exposure time (1/30s) smears a 2 m/s mosquito across 40–50 pixels.
  • Latency Bottleneck: Running TinyML object detection on an ESP32-S3 achieves only 3–5 FPS with 200ms latency, making real-time interception impossible.
