Camera and AI board: FireBeetle 2 ESP32-S3

Indicative listed price: ~’1,949

DetailValue
IncludesCamera and wireless connectivity
Relationship to LiteWingSeparate board — not confirmed as a plug-and-play upgrade
RoleExperimental starting point for embedded-vision experiments

Before purchasing, confirm:

  • Weight
  • Dimensions
  • Power requirements
  • Camera interface
  • Software support
  • Communication method
  • How camera video can be accessed by AI software
  • Whether frame rate is adequate
Capability caveat: The ESP32-S3 can support lightweight embedded-vision experiments, but dependable recognition of tiny, fast-moving mosquitoes may require a more capable AI computer. Treat this board as an experimental starting point, not a proven mosquito-recognition solution.
